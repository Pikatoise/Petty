# Dachshund

Original procedural C# pixel art, derived from experiments/pixel-pets.
No third-party sprite pixels or AI-generated art. Source: assets/breed-designs.json and BreedSpriteRenderer.
64px cells, 32px logical grid, nearest-neighbour 2x, binary alpha, ground row 53.
Nine shared clips including petting; 64 frames per coat. First coat is the creator default.

- `dachshund-red`: solid
- `dachshund-black-tan`: tricolor
- `dachshund-chocolate-tan`: tricolor
- `dachshund-silver-dapple`: dapple
- `dachshund-brindle`: brindle

Stylized anatomy; species share gait structure with breed-specific proportions, ears, tails and head shapes.
Visual review of motion/ball contact and DPI remains an owner check.
